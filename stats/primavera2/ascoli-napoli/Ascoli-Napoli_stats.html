Data quality

  • Tagged from a single wide panoramic camera at 360p quality, with no broadcast feed, replays or clock overlay.
  • Most players were identified by their position in the team shape rather than by readable shirt numbers, so individual figures are low confidence; team totals are more reliable. Events where no player could be identified are listed as “player unclear”.
  • xG is an estimate from a simple distance/angle model (penalties fixed at 0.76), not a trained model.
  • One Ascoli long-range shot (Di Mattia, 90:57) has an uncertain distance, and some late events have uncertain details (e.g. an unattributed Ascoli offside at 93:44).
  • Ascoli's 82' triple substitution (Cicchitti, Balducci, Martinelli) was not visible on camera and is timed from the match sheet (82:30).
  • Two Napoli shots have no visible outcome and are counted as not on target. Ascoli's 04:29 shot by Dente G. is counted as on target because Spinelli is seen saving it.
